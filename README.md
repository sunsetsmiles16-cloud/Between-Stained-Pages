# Between Stained Pages 📖✨

"A family recipe is a timeline of love, written down on index cards, stained with coffee, vanilla, and memory."

**Between Stained Pages** is an heirloom-grade, retro-styled culinary archive web application. Designed with a peaceful, muted earthy aesthetic, it allows home cooks to preserve handwritten family index cards, digital screenshots, and web URLs into custom-bound 3D walnut cookbooks.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **Multi-Decade Design System:** Toggle seamlessly across 10 distinct historical culinary eras (1920s Art Deco through 2010s Millennial Minimal), changing the app's typography, color palettes, and card styles dynamically.
* **Multi-Page Card & Screenshot OCR:** Powered by Google Gemini AI, upload up to 3 pages of a handwritten recipe card or continuous screenshot to transcribe ingredients and instructions automatically. Client-side canvas compression ensures zero serverless payload timeouts.
* **3D Walnut Bookshelf:** A skeuomorphic library where custom-bound cookbooks sit on rich walnut shelves complete with interactive vertical spines, custom cloth colors, font styling, and food emoji badges (🥐, 🍕, 🍰, 🍳, etc.).
* **Fleshed-Out Kitchen Pantry:** Track home staple ingredients (Eggs, Milk, Butter, Flour, Sugar, Salt, Olive Oil) organized by grocery categories with auto-checking and live inventory counters.
* **Kitchen Reference & Substitutions:** Built-in substitution database (e.g., Greek yogurt for sour cream, buttermilk equivalents) with instant search.
* **Accessibility & Scaling:** Live text-accessibility size slider and instant Imperial-to-Metric unit conversion toggles.
* **Secure Cloud & Guest Modes:** Authenticate via Supabase for cross-device cloud synchronization (phone, iPad, and web) with guest-session warnings and one-click JSON data backups.
